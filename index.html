01 / The NewMed products

Two working products, built end to end.

Both are fully working prototypes: real SQL over a synthetic claims warehouse, a model trained and evaluated offline, and interactive UIs where every number recomputes live. No screenshots, no slideware.

Flagship · Claims & billing AUC 0.853 · 80% preventable · $46k/mo protected

Denial Prevention Co-Pilot

US practices see 10 to 15% of claims denied, at $25 to $118 of rework each, and nearly half are never recovered. Most causes are visible before submission. The Co-Pilot scores every claim at the last controllable moment, explains why in biller language, predicts the denial code, and offers a one-click fix with undo. The biller decides; every action feeds the model.

Companion · Practice operations 3,324 h/mo · 20.8 FTEs · 69% automatable

Practice Ops Bottleneck Analyzer

Practices know they are busy; they do not know where the hours go. This mines 13 weeks of operational task events, ranks each practice's manual-effort sinks, and pairs every bottleneck with a specific automation recommendation and a quantified saving, with a what-if slider for adoption.
