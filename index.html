FLAGSHIP · CLAIMS & BILLING

Denial Prevention Co-Pilot

US practices see 10 to 15% of insurance claims denied, at $25 to $118 of rework each, and nearly half are never recovered. Most denial causes are visible before submission. The Co-Pilot scores every claim at the last controllable moment, explains why in biller language, predicts the denial code, and offers a one-click fix with undo. The biller decides; every action feeds the model.

The method is the point

Healthcare was a new domain for me; the way in is always the same. Every step below is visible inside the products, not claimed in a deck.

STEP 1

Start in the data, with SQL

Eight discovery queries over a 6,000-claim warehouse decided whether to build at all: how big, where it concentrates, what share is preventable, and whether the signal is visible in time. Every query and result ships inside the app, with clause-level comments.

STEP 2

Ship an interpretable model

A 15-weight logistic model a biller can audit, trained on the past and tested on the future (temporal backtest, AUC 0.853). The operating threshold is exposed as a product decision: biller workload against denials caught, with live economics.

STEP 3

Gate the rollout with evals

Offline backtest, then a gated alpha with 100% human review, then payer-first expansion, then GA against clean-claim-rate lift vs control practices. Overrides are one click and become training data. Trust is earned in stages.
